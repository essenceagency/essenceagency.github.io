# Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

This policy covers Essence Agency and its shops and tools, including the WanderPixelCo Etsy shop and the WanderPixelCo Pin Scheduler app ("we", "us").

## What we collect

When you buy from WanderPixelCo on Etsy, Etsy collects and processes your order and payment details under Etsy's own privacy policy. We receive the order information we need to make and ship your item: your name, shipping address, the items you ordered and any message you send us.

Our Pinterest app only connects to our own Pinterest account. It creates Pins on our own boards and reads our own boards. It does not collect information about other Pinterest users.

This website does not use cookies or tracking.

## How we use it

We use order information only to fulfill your order, provide customer service, handle returns and replacements, and keep business and tax records.

## Who we share it with

We share only what is needed with the services that run our business: Etsy, where you place your order and pay; Printify and its print providers, who print and ship your order; Pinterest, where we publish Pins about our own products; and AI service providers (Anthropic and OpenAI), which we use to create designs and to help draft replies to customer messages. When we use AI to draft a reply, the content of your message may be processed by these providers.

We never sell your personal information.

## How long we keep it

We keep order records as long as needed for customer service, returns and legal or tax requirements, and then delete them.

## Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us. For data held by Etsy, you can also use Etsy's privacy settings.
